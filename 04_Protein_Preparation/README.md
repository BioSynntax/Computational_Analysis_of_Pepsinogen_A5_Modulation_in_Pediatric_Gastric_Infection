# Pepsinogen A5 Activation and Protein Preparation

## Overview

This section describes the computational activation of human Pepsinogen A5 and subsequent preparation of the activated structure for molecular docking analysis.

## Objective

The objective was to computationally model the activation of Pepsinogen A5 into its active pepsin-like form and prepare the resulting structure under acidic gastric conditions for downstream molecular docking.

## Computational Activation

Pepsinogen A5 is synthesized as an inactive zymogen. To computationally model its activation, the N-terminal 44 amino acid residues were removed from the modeled Pepsinogen A5 structure.

This generated an activated pepsin-like protein structure for subsequent computational analysis.

## Acidic pH Preparation

Following removal of the N-terminal activation segment, the activated structure was prepared under acidic conditions corresponding to approximately pH 2, representing the gastric environment.

The protonation states of relevant residues were adjusted accordingly.

## Energy Minimization

Energy minimization was subsequently performed to reduce unfavorable steric interactions and obtain an energetically optimized activated protein structure.

## Workflow

1. Human Pepsinogen A5 sequence/model selection
2. Removal of the N-terminal 44 amino acids
3. Generation of the activated pepsin-like structure
4. Protonation under acidic conditions (pH 2)
5. Energy minimization
6. Structural inspection of the activated protein
7. Preparation of the activated structure as the docking receptor

## Final Protein Structure

The minimized activated Pepsinogen A5 structure was used as the receptor for molecular docking with the selected phytocompounds.

## Software Used

- MODELLER
- PyMOL
- Discovery Studio
- Molecular structure preparation and minimization tools

## Output

The final output of this stage was an activated and energy-minimized Pepsinogen A5 structure prepared for molecular docking analysis.

## Note

The activation process represents a computational model of Pepsinogen A5 activation under acidic gastric conditions. The resulting structure is an in silico representation and requires experimental validation.
