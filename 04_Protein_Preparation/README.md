# Protein Preparation

## Overview

This section describes the computational preparation of the Pepsinogen A5 protein structure for molecular docking analysis.

## Objective

The objective was to prepare the modeled Pepsinogen A5 structure in an activation-relevant state and obtain an optimized protein structure suitable for ligand docking.

## Protein Activation

Pepsinogen A5 is synthesized as an inactive zymogen. For computational analysis, the N-terminal 44 amino acid residues were removed to generate the activated pepsin-like structure.

## pH Preparation

The activated protein structure was prepared under acidic conditions corresponding to the gastric environment. Protonation states were adjusted to represent approximately pH 2 conditions.

## Energy Minimization

Energy minimization was performed to relieve unfavorable steric interactions and obtain a structurally optimized protein conformation suitable for subsequent molecular docking.

## Workflow

1. Selection of the modeled Pepsinogen A5 structure
2. Removal of the N-terminal 44 amino acid residues
3. Preparation under acidic pH conditions
4. Addition/adjustment of hydrogen atoms and protonation states
5. Energy minimization
6. Structural inspection of the prepared protein
7. Preparation of the final docking-ready structure

## Output

The resulting optimized and activation-state protein structure was used as the receptor for molecular docking with selected bioactive compounds.

## Tools Used

* PyMOL
* Discovery Studio
* Molecular structure preparation and minimization tools

## Note

The activation and protonation states represent a computational approximation of the acidic gastric environment. These structural modifications are intended for in silico analysis and require experimental validation.
