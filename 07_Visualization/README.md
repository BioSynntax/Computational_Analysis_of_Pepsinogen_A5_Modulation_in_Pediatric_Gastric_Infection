# Visualization

## Overview

This section contains the structural and molecular interaction visualizations generated during the computational analysis of Pepsinogen A5 and its selected phytocompounds.

## Objective

The objective of this section is to visually examine the three-dimensional protein structure and predicted ligand–protein interactions obtained from molecular docking.

## Visualization Analysis

The following aspects are represented:

- Three-dimensional structure of Pepsinogen A5
- Docked ligand–protein complexes
- Hydrogen-bond interactions
- Interacting amino acid residues
- Binding-site interactions
- Molecular surface and structural representations

## Software Used

- PyMOL
- Discovery Studio

## Molecular Interaction Visualization

Discovery Studio was used to visualize and analyze predicted ligand–protein interactions, including hydrogen bonds and other non-covalent interactions.

PyMOL was used for three-dimensional visualization of the protein structure and docked complexes.

## Output

The visualization files provide graphical representations of the predicted molecular interactions and structural features identified during the computational analysis.

## Note

The displayed molecular interactions represent computational docking predictions and should not be considered experimentally confirmed interactions.
